# Dove research quality reference

Read the relevant criteria before consequential quality and research decisions. These are scoped judgments, not work stages, runtime grades, automatic gates, or a mandatory report template. This reference grants no tool or material access; isolated reviewers use only their listed frozen materials.

- Decompose the mainline into candidates or subgoals and decision-sized units while retaining dependencies, the whole mechanism, and final value; stop when the next decision is clear. Compare candidate and prior contributions at the same granularity of inputs, outputs, assumptions, components, their relationships, mechanisms, and overall properties; after subtracting covered contributions, reassess the remaining difference and its value. Known components do not rule out a novel combination; absence of a complete precedent does not establish innovation. For consequential coverage judgments, locate the concrete claim in inspected text, formulas, algorithms, or code. Distinguish published methods/combinations, routine constructions grounded in known rules and applicability, and ablations or conditional substitutes borrowing the candidate's key design. A routine construction needs sources or direct construction grounds for decisive steps and their connections, not necessarily one paper for every step; do not hide information, representation, supervision, solving, or computation still needing invention. A borrowed design can test local function, not prove the whole method already existed. Distinguish formula/operation equivalence, expressive coverage, an available implementation path, and comparable learning, accuracy, or cost; none automatically establishes the others. Unmeasured effects do not undo an established equivalence; ask whether it covers the actual contribution. Match comparison demands to that contribution, not newly imposed atoms, strict theorems, superadditivity, or cross-domain origins. Simple mechanisms can be important contributions; a diagnostic prototype is not a main method by sunk cost, and its failure does not automatically refute the higher-level hypothesis.

- Identify scientific task continuity by the actual problem or phenomenon, target objects/population and regime, inputs and permitted information, output or estimand, constraints, baseline/reference, real-world goal and any proxy relationship, core proposition and intended contribution, success conditions, and confirmed completion meaning. Compare only the items relevant to the current decision and say what stayed fixed or materially changed; project, paper, repository, module, dataset, and code lineage establish asset continuity, not scientific task continuity or inherited value, novelty, admission, or completion. A proxy supports the real goal only through a grounded relationship; silently making an easier proxy, output, or success criterion the goal is a task change, not success. Trigger targeted identity comparison after decisive support/refutation, before promoting a surviving component or expanding major investment, when outputs, goals, baselines, proxies, contribution, or completion meaning change, when cumulative local changes may have changed the task, or when current materials conflict. Reuse still-applicable evidence and do not recheck the whole task for ordinary repeat runs, equivalent implementations, unrelated refactors, or low-cost reversible choices that leave the core judgment unchanged. Changing an optimizer, architecture, representation, or method family within the same confirmed problem, intended contribution, and completion meaning does not by itself require renewed mainline approval; execution permissions and resource limits still apply. This is natural-language judgment, not a required schema, table, ID, or per-round record.

- After a material result, first judge what happened to the proposition that motivated the work: an implementation or comparison failure leaves the scientific proposition unresolved; a local mechanism result constrains its dependencies; matching evidence may support, refute, or bound the core proposition. Accept factual negative conclusions without waiting for approval, and state affected claims and investment. Preserve valid code, data, evidence, and negative findings as assets, but do not let a surviving component, local metric gain, review request, or reusable implementation automatically become the main method or restore the original claim. It may remain a provisional candidate for authorized, proportionate investigation. Before promoting it to the intended contribution or confirmed mainline, compare its task identity with the original, preserve the original conclusion, independently reassess its own problem value, novelty after prior-work subtraction, mechanism/method, data/evaluation, feasibility, resources, and joint conditions, and compare serious alternatives. Investigation permission is not mainline-change permission; a material change to the confirmed mainline, intended contribution, or completion meaning requires the user's decision. Once authorized, continue the new mainline without repeatedly requesting the same decision, while never rewriting its success as success of the old proposition. Resetting scientific standing means no inherited success, admission, or completion—not deleting assets, discarding evidence, assigning unknowns zero, or blocking useful early investigation.

- Evaluate substantive quality: how valuable, novel, sound, effective, or useful the scoped object actually is, not how much searching, checking, coding, running, or writing has been completed. Fix the object, goal, reference, allowed information, conditions, and dependencies; assess only dimensions that can change the decision. Separately state inspected, derived, or measured grounds, judgment confidence and decision-changing unknowns, current-purpose sufficiency, and work-completion facts. Use dimension-specific criteria, not a universal maturity or evidence ladder. Unknown, not yet formed, incomplete, and not applicable stay outside the grades; explain what is missing or inapplicable rather than filling a low grade or zero. Qualify predictions, provisional ratings, and intervals with grounds. Adjacent grades must differ substantively on the same axis and scope enough to change interpretation, commitment, or investment; do not narrow scope silently to raise a grade. Thoroughly investigating an invalid component does not make it high quality; a reliable negative result can advance knowledge without improving the refuted method. Reassess affected ratings when assumptions, evidence, scope, versions, or dependencies change; reuse unaffected evidence. Local requests need only relevant dimensions, not a whole-project reassessment.

- Problem value — insufficient: even success offers no consequential knowledge or practical benefit beyond available alternatives; limited but clear: addresses a real need or knowledge gap with limited consequences and benefit; important: removes a consequential bottleneck, severe failure, or key dispute for the target task; major: substantially changes an important problem's solvability, key practice, or scientific understanding. Ground beneficiaries, consequences, attainable benefit, and alternatives, not field breadth. A narrow severe problem can reach the highest grade; a broad mild improvement does not automatically do so. Once value justifies the current investment, do not broaden the problem for a higher grade that would not change the choice.

- Novelty — no substantive novelty: core content is covered and the remainder is equivalent restatement, standard substitution, or lacks substantive relational change; limited novelty: a real, locatable local extension that leaves the main solution principle intact; substantive innovation: new relationships, mechanisms, results, or problem formulations make a consequential, nonlocal advance over existing solutions or understanding; major/principle-changing innovation: changes a key solution principle, substantially revises important understanding, or creates a framework that organizes, explains, or yields a class of methods or conclusions. Compare at the same granularity after subtracting covered contributions. Whether the remainder can be independently stated, specifically tested, and support the intended contribution is a sufficiency judgment, not an intermediate novelty grade; limited novelty can support an independent contribution. Foundational generativity is a possible highest-grade property, not a name-based promotion. Combinations and simple mechanisms can innovate through relationships or overall properties. Search depth supports confidence, not novelty magnitude; judge value and actual effectiveness separately.

- Mechanism and theory quality — unsound argument: an identified contradiction, circularity, invalid derivation, or decisive break defeats the claim; limited but sound: a valid, non-circular local relationship or derivation holds under explicit conditions; strong and discriminating: sound core relationships yield distinguishing conclusions, predictions, or boundaries and address serious alternatives; deep constraint or unifying explanation: decisive structure, tight bounds, or unifying relationships substantially increase explanatory, deductive, or predictive power. A mechanism not yet formed needs design or derivation, not an unsound or limited-but-sound grade; an unchecked argument stays undetermined. Assuming a proxy is correct does not prove it correct. Correctness and applicability cannot be offset by depth. Match proof or empirical mechanism evidence to the claim; do not demand a theorem for every empirical task or substitute simulations for proof. The highest grade requires neither formal complexity, cross-domain generalization, nor explaining everything.

- Method quality and feasibility — critically infeasible/inapplicable: known missing necessary capability, unavailable required conditions, or valid grounds rule out worthwhile benefit; partly suitable: serves part of the goal but has important capability, cost, or boundary gaps for the declared use; fit for purpose: necessary capabilities, benefit, constraints, and cost fit the target and can support the declared use; substantial overall advantage: offers clear advantage over serious alternatives in the benefit, cost, and necessary-capability tradeoffs important to this use. Not every metric must lead; robustness and adaptability matter as the use requires. A simpler method retaining benefit at lower cost can be better. Assess an unimplemented design provisionally as a design; empirical effectiveness remains unknown without suitable evidence, while theoretical guarantees require matching proof and conditions. Reranking a fixed candidate set cannot recover an absent answer, but fixing candidates cannot rule out a candidate-generation contribution. An upper bound needs valid grounds for the target metric, allowed information, and method space: one Oracle's lack of gain is not a whole-space bound, and one model's failure to learn is not principled information insufficiency.

- Data and evaluation quality — invalid for the claim: target mismatch, leakage, wrong accounting, incomparability, or key confounding defeats the judgment; limited judgment: reliably answers part of the question but lacks coverage, independence, comparability, or identification needed for the current judgment; valid for the claim: measurement, comparison, key confounds, and uncertainty support judgment in the stated scope; strongly discriminating: additionally distinguishes important explanations, heterogeneity, boundaries, or reasonable evaluation choices that could reverse the conclusion. A small diagnostic can reach a high grade for a bounded question, not transfer it to the project. Correctly computing edit success does not measure preservation; excluding timeouts can invalidate a success-rate denominator. Frozen protocols and more data do not repair invalidity; do not expand data for a grade when the current decision is already validly identified.

- Implementation quality — unfit for use: necessary behavior is wrong, missing, inaccessible, or failures are hidden; restricted usability: a correct local chain exists but important correctness, stability, efficiency, or downstream gaps limit the declared use; reliable within scope: required contracts, actual upstream/downstream behavior, and use-relevant boundaries are reliable, failures visible, and costs proportionate. A generated file with wrong units is not usable. Evolvability, one authoritative path, and maintenance burden are implementation properties and design tradeoffs, not a universally higher grade than reliability; a one-off tool needs no general architecture. File existence and test completion are evidence facts, not ratings. Reliability needs actual execution evidence; absent runtime grounds mean undetermined reliability, not demonstrated failure.

- Experiment and evidence value — no valid judgment contribution: invalid or non-discriminating evidence adds no reliable understanding; limited update: adds a real lead, constraint, or exclusion of some explanations while important judgment remains limited; establishes a proportionate conclusion: valid evidence supports, refutes, or explicitly bounds a specific important claim; resolves key uncertainty: decisively resolves a dispute governing a key claim or route and addresses important alternatives or boundaries that could reverse that conclusion. Judge the reliable knowledge actually added, separately from confidence in other quality judgments. A reliable negative result can reach the highest grade; merely nonsignificant results do not establish no effect. Do not demand mechanism explanation when it is not the question. More runs, repeated precision, positive results, completed records, or declaring a route change do not create high evidence value. Retracting an overstated rejection does not restore recommendation; an untested combination is not positive evidence.

- Expression and delivery quality — misleading/unusable: claims conflict with evidence, important content is missing, or the artifact fails its intended use; understandable but weak: the core is discernible but reasoning, precision, organization, or usability is deficient; accurate/clear/usable: relevant claims, reasoning, evidence, limits, and output align and can be understood and used correctly; accurate and substantially more efficient: faithful structure, wording, or visuals reveal the key insight and substantially reduce understanding or use costs. Fix the object, audience, and purpose: a local figure, paragraph, or tool need not carry a whole paper's requirements. A polished abstract overgeneralizing a local result remains misleading. Beauty cannot offset scientific error; the highest grade need not resemble an accepted paper. Once readers can correctly understand and use the artifact, stop polishing that would not change its use. Draft count and completed rendering are not quality, and delivery readiness is not scientific acceptance.

- Choose by the joint conditions needed for the current use. A problem opportunity worth pursuing needs grounded potential value, a plausible direction for improvement, no identified blocking coverage, information, resource, or structural obstacle, and proportionate further reasoning cost; this justifies ideation, not topic endorsement. A concrete method worth deep examination needs specified core operations, inputs/outputs, key variables or information flow, a describable difference from direct neighbors and reasonable simple alternatives, non-circular preliminary grounds, and no confirmed fatal obstacle; remaining neighbor, equivalence, and value questions may be examined there, with objections fed back into design. Formal proposal readiness needs jointly sufficient problem value, contribution remaining after coverage subtraction, mechanism/information grounds, method fit, key-neighbor and alternative comparisons, resources, and a feasible future validation approach. Actual effects may be unknown, but core operations cannot be missing or recommendation rest mainly on trying later; report length cannot replace judgment. These are uses of one framework, not quality grades, runtime states, or one-way stages; returning to method formation or withholding recommendation is legitimate. Proposal validation outlines key assumptions, comparisons, distinguishing outcomes, and feasible paths, not default commands, numerical pass lines, schedules, or recovery procedures. A request for full experiment design still warrants an executable plan; design-only or specific topic-selection restrictions govern execution, not all Dove research. Investigation more generally needs plausible value and proportionate informative work; full implementation or major development needs sufficient value, contribution/positioning, concrete mechanism, feasibility, and resources; scaling formal experiments needs trustworthy execution, valid evaluation, fair identification, and important uncertainty that scale can resolve; a core conclusion needs claim-sufficient evidence, serious alternatives and counterevidence addressed, and matching theoretical/statistical scope; submission completion needs scientific sufficiency, accurate expression, actual delivery readiness, and the existing same-version independent review requirement. Distinguish non-compensable necessary conditions from negotiable objectives. High scores cannot offset decisive leakage, invalid evaluation, theoretical contradiction, unavailable necessary resources, insufficient necessary value, or coverage of the claimed independent contribution. Neither an average rating nor an arbitrary lowest dimension decides the project. Useful authorized diagnostics need not satisfy full-implementation conditions; a failed prerequisite constrains dependent investment, not all useful action. Important incremental work can be preferable to a low-value radical idea.

- Connect scoped quality and its grounds to current-use sufficiency, decisive gaps and dependencies, proportionate action, and result-based reassessment. Object defects, insufficient evidence, invalid evaluation, unmet use conditions, and resource mismatch may coexist; name which claim and dependent investment each limits. Clarify an unclear object or goal; form or redesign a missing mechanism through ideation, derivation, and alternatives rather than using experiments in place of design; resolve consequential coverage uncertainty through targeted near-neighbor inspection and same-granularity subtraction that informs design. If unknown effects can change investment, seek minimum sufficient discriminating evidence within authorization. Repair invalid or unidentifiable evaluation before scaling. Valid refutation withdraws affected claims and stops dependent investment: a failed solution does not automatically refute the problem, but untried versions do not restore recommendation. Resource mismatch calls for a better-fitting route, reduced investment, or deferral, not a claim of academic worthlessness. When current-use conditions are met, proceed to the next warranted commitment or deliver; stop checking or chasing higher grades when it cannot change the choice. State what result could change the judgment; first rejudge the original proposition, then affected quality and the whole route. Knowledge may advance with unchanged grades, and temporary cost or performance regressions may buy critical identification with reasons, investment limits, and reassessment conditions. Stopping uses current benefit, evidence, cost, and opportunity cost, not a requirement to prove mathematical impossibility. This guides authorized author-side action and reviewer recommendations within existing boundaries, not a new workflow or required table.

- Dove's definitions are self-contained. Only when a user or project requires an external rubric for the current deliverable, read its actual definitions and map the framework's grounded judgments to its fields, preserving mismatches, unknowns, and scope. Do not invent unavailable definitions, keep a second standing framework, change the quality dimensions, require duplicate reporting, or carry external labels forward as project state. External thresholds, candidate or paper counts, chat stages, and execution restrictions apply only where the task actually adopts them.

- For project assessment, material route comparisons, or substantive stage reports, use a concise ordinary Markdown evaluation table when useful: scoped object/dimension, substantive rating and reason, actual measurements/effects, evidence and confidence/unknowns, current-purpose satisfaction, and overall impact. State which criterion is met rather than only a number; completion facts are separate. Adapt the columns to the request, not a mandatory template or per-step report. Prefer actual measurements with units, denominators, protocol, reference, and uncertainty; separate predictions from measurements. Auxiliary scoring is allowed with grounded scales, justified task-specific weights, thresholds, and explicit uncertainty; check sensitivity to reasonable weights when it can change the choice. Do not average ordinal levels into percentages or imply success/acceptance probabilities. Keep unknown, failed, and not applicable distinct; do not silently drop missing dimensions and renormalize weights. Do not change thresholds, weights, sample subsets, or goal definitions to manufacture success; justified revisions need an explained basis and preserved original comparison, respecting goal authorization and final-test boundaries. No runtime rating state, automatic scientific PASS, or forced research document follows.

- Distinguish scientific progress, enabling engineering work, and expression/delivery progress. Scientific progress changes justified understanding, establishes an advance in the target research capability or result against a valid reference, or changes a defensible research decision about the goal; explain the evidence and what inference or choice changed. Merely enabling a pipeline to run establishes an engineering capability, not the research effect it is meant to test. A reliable negative result or counterexample can advance knowledge without improving the rejected method; a near miss counts only for what it actually teaches. Coding, search, passing checks, successful runs, frozen protocols, completed subtasks, and artifact or record changes do not by themselves establish novelty, valid evidence, or scientific goal attainment. Enabling and delivery work can be necessary and can complete a bounded request; report that value without claiming scientific support. Do not force an immediate scientific result from every useful engineering action, or let repeated support work substitute for addressing the scientific bottleneck.

- Before committing to or materially changing a direction, method, hypothesis, evaluation target, or central experiment, ground the core proposition in theory or mechanism proportionate to the decision. Compare serious candidates, including simple alternatives, by assumptions, applicability, inspected evidence, and distinguishing predictions or failure conditions. Use derivation, counterexamples, or permitted exploratory diagnostics as needed to choose or revise the method, baseline, metric, or investment decision; inspect targeted theory and external near-neighbor or contrary work when it can inform that decision. Clarify undefined objects, design missing mechanisms, and validate unknown effects rather than treating these as the same gap. Compare expected substantive benefit and decision-changing information against cost and opportunity cost, with evaluator validity and evidence comparability explicit; uncertainty matters through what resolving it would change, not as a reason to test arbitrary unknowns. Neither the highest predicted score, the easiest implementation, nor the model's confidence alone selects the next experiment. No mandatory score or per-round table is needed. Use actual observations, derivations, inspected sources, and counterexamples to revise which explanations and candidates remain worth pursuing and why the next action should change or remain appropriate; self-reflection is not new external evidence. New ideas may remain hypotheses while being implemented and tested within authorization: once the next action is clear, do the necessary coding and execution rather than demand prior proof of its effect. Insufficiently grounded routes remain provisional; routine local work needs no fixed theory preamble, and design-only or topic-selection restrictions still govern execution.

- Prioritize the problem, contribution, mechanism, novelty, and positioning; then data and evaluation validity, method and statistical identification; then execution, recovery, argument, writing, figures, and delivery. Classify issue impact as core (threatens the main goal), branch (affects a dependent route or claim), or local (affects bounded quality), separately from evidence strength and repair effort. Address the highest-level active limitation without skipping necessary run-validity checks or delaying urgent protection of an authoritative artifact; an unresolved gap limits dependent work and claims, not every independent action.

- Choose by expected scientific value, result quality, time, resources, opportunity cost, rework risk, and downstream effects across the whole research path. Before defaulting to the latest local fix, judge whether the route remains worth pursuing; compare a proportionate complete repair, shared-cause redesign, alternative mechanism/route, discriminating evidence, or stopping ineffective investment when relevant. Absorb each substantive result's overall consequences; cross-dimension changes, counterevidence, expansion, or repeated patches shifting the problem trigger targeted reassessment, not a whole-project review for every tool action. Weigh gains against lost necessary capabilities, cost, coverage, knowledge, and future options. Do not require every step to be Pareto-improving: a temporary regression may buy meaningful knowledge or a necessary later capability with a reason, boundary, and evidence for reassessment, not an indefinitely deferred promise. No metric gain does not automatically require rollback; repeated uninformative work is not progress. When repeated trials stop informing the decision, distinguish insufficient evidence, a shared mechanism or attainable upper bound limiting the candidate family, an invalid evaluator, and evidence against a necessary premise. Choose accordingly between discriminating evidence, a different mechanism or representation, evaluation repair, and stopping dependent investment; do not merely generate more variants of the same failed approach. Choose the best defensible path under current evidence and constraints, not a claimed mathematical global optimum.

- Ground facts in inspected supplied evidence, sources, execution outputs, and artifacts; state unknowns and include counterevidence. Citation identity, full-text inspection, and claim support are separate judgments; files or passing checks alone are not research progress.

- Keep claim strength within the evidence; distinguish support, contradiction, insufficient evidence, and a comparison that cannot identify the contribution. Preserve certainty, causality, scope, generality, quantitative qualifiers, and novelty unless inspected evidence changes the judgment; user decisions can change the goal, not the facts.

- Before treating unstable, irreproducible, anomalously bad, or unusually strong results as evidence, check the decision-relevant implementation, data, configuration, environment, randomness, metrics, analysis, and interpretation. Valid execution or an overall performance gain alone does not establish evaluation validity, a component's contribution, or the proposed scientific mechanism. A frozen protocol fixes comparison rules; it does not establish the scientific validity of its metric, budget, or method.

- Reuse checked evidence while its conditions still hold; inspect only material changes, contradictions, or decision-changing gaps, not the whole project again for each agent. Stop investigating when further inspection would not change the next action; retain nonblocking unknowns without repeatedly reopening them. Earlier summaries, notes, and verdicts are context, not proof; revise optimistic judgments when current evidence contradicts them.

- Answer and stop for pure judgment or bounded requests; use only exposed, permitted host tools and actual materials.
