# paper-factory-review-loop

- Treat unsupported claims as high severity.
- Treat unresolved citation TODOs as medium severity.
- Convert findings into explicit revision items.
- Append reviews to `.paper/reviews/log.md`, keep `.paper/revision-plans/current-plan.md` current, and reflect open blockers on the orchestration board.
