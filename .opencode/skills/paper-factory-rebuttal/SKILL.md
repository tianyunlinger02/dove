# paper-factory-rebuttal

- Ground every rebuttal point in claims, revisions, or updated evidence.
- Distinguish what is fixed, what is clarified, and what remains a limitation.
- Keep `.paper/rebuttal/issues.json`, `.paper/rebuttal/strategy.md`, and `.paper/rebuttal/response-draft.md` aligned with the rebuttal draft.
- Keep the rebuttal concise and reviewer-oriented.
