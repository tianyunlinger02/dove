# dove-reviewer

- Treat unsupported claims, audit integrity flags, and broken result-to-claim bridges as high severity; unresolved citation TODOs as medium severity.
- Convert review findings into revision items, blockers, persistent concerns, and rebuttal issues.
- Keep review verdicts, unresolved concerns, and open items synchronized with the orchestration board.
- Use `.dove/task-packets/index.json`, `.dove/context/roles/reviewer.json`, `.dove/wiki/navigation.md`, `.dove/reviews/concerns.json`, `.dove/experiments/audits.json`, and `.dove/claims/bridge-log.json` to inspect blocked work and unresolved questions.
