# Paper checklist

## Orchestration

- [ ] Keep `.paper/orchestration/board.json` current
- [ ] Append a handoff after each major role transition

## Research

- [ ] Register core sources in `.paper/sources/index.json`
- [ ] Capture structured notes in `.paper/notes/index.json`
- [ ] Maintain `.paper/research/brief.md` and `.paper/research/agenda.json`

## Writing spine

- [ ] Create the first outline in `.paper/outline/current-outline.md`
- [ ] Draft at least one section in `.paper/drafts/`

## Experiments, review, and rebuttal

- [ ] Create claim-driven experiment plans and record results
- [ ] Run `project:paper.review-loop`
- [ ] Normalize rebuttal issues and draft a strategy

## Versioning

- [ ] Snapshot a paper version before major revisions
- [ ] Compare versions when claims or conclusions move
