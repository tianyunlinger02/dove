# Writing and review

Researcher-owned, optional guidance; use the requested writing or review scope. See [Lessons](LESSONS.md) for ownership and advice maturity.

## Build an argument, not a development history

Organize the paper around an important problem, a specific prior gap, a falsifiable hypothesis, a unified mechanism, fair evidence, and capability boundaries. Explain which obstacle is removed and what is new versus inherited models, public data, tools, simulators, and services. Compare nearest work on actual input/output, information, supervision, deployment, budget, mechanism, and user need, not just technique names.

The title, abstract, first figure, and opening should quickly establish the problem, why existing methods are insufficient, the core mechanism, nearest-work difference, and decisive evidence when available. The method explains both how and why: task and deployment, variables and information flow, mechanism, training objective, inference, constraints, complexity, and failure conditions. Do not hide search, postprocessing, human steps, or external tools.

Organize experiments by questions: fair effectiveness, mechanism attribution, information and budget, generalization, cost, and failure. Analyze with judgment, evidence, and explanation rather than repeating tables. Separate data generalization, method applicability, and reusable principles; interface compatibility, external literature, and hoped-for extensions are not current system evidence.

Consolidate common limitations and unfinished-work notices; state assumptions near the claim when they directly determine validity. Core novelty, definition, or mechanism gaps must change the recommendation, not disappear into caveats. A claim-changing gap needs substantive work, whereas normal unknown effects need a validation plan. Do not weaken a sound claim merely to sound cautious or preserve an unsupported one through repeated qualification.

Distinguish an argument outline, complete draft, evidence-consistent manuscript, and manuscript meeting actual delivery requirements. Writing completion is independent of scientific maturity; a polished proposal can lack effect evidence and a complete draft can still contain a core scientific gap. Local prose edits preserve claim meaning and do not restart research. Use stable terminology, plain language, and evidence rather than marketing adjectives.

## Figures and actual delivery

Organize each figure or panel around its explanatory or evidential task, gathering the needed source materials before drawing and captioning. Separate schematic illustration from measured evidence. Distinguish an expression plan, generated visual, checked materials and meaning, and checked final use context. Source code or a standalone preview alone does not establish legibility, correct embedding, or consistency with neighboring claims in the actual manuscript. Keep local changes bounded.

Preserve the authoritative manuscript format and actual venue requirements. Delivery inspection covers the current built/exported version and necessary submission materials; file validity and formatting do not prove scientific acceptability.

## Review and response

Review problem value, novelty, correctness, identification, evidence, external validity, reproducibility, clarity, and reader/venue fit. Use actual official requirements and inspected nearest work rather than claiming that a project preference is a conference rule. Report the strongest reasonable objection and what could resolve it.

Classify issues as core, branch, or local by the goal and dependencies they constrain, not repair effort, and state evidence sufficiency separately. A local check remains local; isolated whole-paper review judges the current complete frozen materials. Author self-check is not independent reviewer acceptance. Reviewer isolation does not grant web, execution, or unlisted Lessons access.

Merge same-root comments and locate definition, mechanism, evidence, or expression gaps. Distinguish concern understood, response path grounded, necessary revision implemented, and effect checked. A written answer is not problem resolution; compare original claim, reviewer understanding, response, and revised claim. Preserve actual reviewer returns separately from author handling. Unresolved core objections must constrain dependent investment and conclusions or be rejected with inspected evidence, not simply left in an old report.
