# Lessons

Lessons are researcher-owned, fallible, reviewable guidance for reuse when relevant. They are not research evidence, scientific validation, permission, or a completion certificate. The existing themes below are local reference material, not package-managed defaults; installation, `dove update`, and SessionStart must preserve them. Read or maintain the relevant advice on demand, not the whole collection by default. Core Dove judgment must remain reachable without Lessons.

Distinguish untested advice, advice grounded in actual sources or observations, and advice checked through reuse under explicit conditions. Keep the basis, applicability, counterexamples, and limits when they matter; repeated citation is not independent validation or automatic promotion. These notes summarize preferences and methods, not a claim that they have all been tested in this repository.

Dove currently has nine flat Skills: Research, Status, Source, Experiment, Draft, Figure, Review, Rebuttal, and Lessons. They are capabilities of one researcher, not personas or a required sequence. The number of local topic documents does not define the capability count.
